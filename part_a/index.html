Biography

I want to understand the engineering principles behind these solutions and become more confident in developing applications. My main goal is to develop a strong foundation in programming, problem-solving, and software development. I want to improve my ability to write clear code, debug errors, and build applications that solve real problems.

In the long term, I hope to secure opportunities in software engineering and combine my knowledge of education and technology to create useful learning tools. I also intend to apply my engineering skills to automation projects that help individuals and businesses work more efficiently.
